About Bella Vista

Our Story

Bella Vista Restaurant was founded in 1985 by Giovanni Rossi in a small hillside town in Italy. Inspired by traditional family recipes, Giovanni started the restaurant to serve authentic Italian food made with fresh, local ingredients.

Named after the beautiful view surrounding the location, Bella Vista began as a small family-run restaurant and is still known today for its warm hospitality and classic Italian flavors.
